🕎 The Sacred Menorah

The Menorah is one of the oldest and most significant symbols in Jewish tradition. This seven-branched golden lampstand was originally placed in the Tabernacle and later in the Temple of Jerusalem. It represents God's eternal presence and divine light.

📜 The Sacred Name - יהוה

The Hebrew letters יהוה (YHWH) represent the sacred Tetragrammaton—the four-letter name of God revealed to Moses. It means "I AM WHO I AM" - the eternal, self-existent nature of God who was, is, and always will be.
