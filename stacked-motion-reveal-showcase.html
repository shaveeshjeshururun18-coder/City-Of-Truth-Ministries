Grammar 04 · Pronominal Attachments 04 / 04

Pronominal Suffixes

பிரதிப்பெயர் பின்னொட்டுகள்

In Biblical Hebrew, pronouns like "my", "your", "his", "our" attach directly to the tail of nouns, verbs, and prepositions to create integrated, covenantal words.

✨ Example: סוּס (Horse) + יִ (My) = סוּסִי (My Horse). בֵּית (House) + נוּ (Our) = בֵּיתֵנוּ (Our House).
Covenant Pronouns
  • ✦ -i (י-) : 1st Person Singular ("My")
  • ✦ -kha (ךָ-) : 2nd Person Masculine ("Your")
  • ✦ -o (וֹ-) : 3rd Person Masculine ("His")
  • ✦ -enu (נוּ-) : 1st Person Plural ("Our")

Grammar 03 · 7 Menorah Verb Patterns 03 / 04

The 7 Binyanim

ஏழு வினைச்சொல் அமைப்புகள் (בִּנְיָנִים)

Seven distinct grammatical stems that mirror the 7 branches of the Golden Menorah. They transform a single root into simple, intensive, causative, and reflexive actions.

✨ The Center Lamp: Hitpa'el (Reflexive Intensive) sits at the center, representing inward transformation, prayer (Hitpallel), and sanctification.
The 7 Stems
  • ✦ Pa'al (Qal) : Simple active (He wrote)
  • ✦ Nif'al : Simple passive / reflexive (It was written)
  • ✦ Pi'el : Intensive active (He shattered)
  • ✦ Pu'al : Intensive passive (It was shattered)
  • ✦ Hif'il : Causative active (He caused to write)
  • ✦ Hof'al : Causative passive (He was made to write)
  • ✦ Hitpa'el : Reflexive intensive (He sanctified himself)

Grammar 02 · Trilateral Roots 02 / 04

The Shoresh Root

முவ்வெழுத்து வேர்ச்சொல் அமைப்பு (שׁוֹרֶשׁ)

Nearly every Hebrew word branches from a 3-consonant seed (Shoresh). Like genetic DNA, the root infuses all nouns, adjectives, and verbs with sacred core meaning.

✨ Example: Root ק-ד-שׁ (Q-D-SH = Holy) yields Kadosh (Holy), Mikdash (Sanctuary), Kiddush (Sanctification), and Kodesh HaKodashim (Holy of Holies).
Root Transformations
  • ✦ ש-ל-ם (SH-L-M) : Shalom (Peace), Shalem (Complete)
  • ✦ ב-ר-ך (B-R-KH) : Berakhah (Blessing), Baruch (Blessed)
  • ✦ מ-ל-ך (M-L-KH) : Melekh (King), Malkhut (Kingdom)

Grammar 01 · Script Foundation 01 / 04

Aleph-Bet & Nikkud

ஆலேப்-பெத் & நிக்குட் புள்ளிகள்

Hebrew is written right-to-left with 22 sacred consonants. Vowel points (Nikkud) are divine dots and dashes added by Masoretes to preserve exact biblical recitation.

📖 Five Final Letters (Sofit): Kaf (ך), Mem (ם), Nun (ן), Pe (ף), and Tsade (ץ) change to majestic straight-line forms when ending a word.
The Primary Vowels
  • ✦ Kamatz / Patakh (ָ / ַ) : "Ah" sound
  • ✦ Tzere / Segol (ֵ / ֶ) : "Eh" sound
  • ✦ Chiriq (ִ) : "Ee" sound
  • ✦ Cholam (ֹ) : "Oh" sound
  • ✦ Shuruk / Kubutz (וּ / ֻ) : "Oo" sound
