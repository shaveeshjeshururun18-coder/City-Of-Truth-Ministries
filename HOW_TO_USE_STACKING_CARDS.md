# 🎯 How to Use the Stacking Cards Feature

## Quick Start Guide

### Step 1: Navigate to Israel Page
1. Open your City of Truth Ministries application
2. Go to the Hebrew section
3. Click on **"Eretz Israel"** or navigate to `/hebrew-israel`

### Step 2: Switch to Stacking View
You'll see two buttons at the top:

```
┌──────────────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────────────┐
│ 📚 Interactive Cards View │  │ 📜 Scrolling Stack View  │
└──────────────────────────┘  └──────────────────────────┘
```

Click on **"📜 Scrolling Stack View"** to activate the new stacking cards experience.

### Step 3: Scroll and Explore
1. Read the hero section: "Israel's Five Regions"
2. Scroll down slowly to see cards stack beautifully
3. Each card represents one region of Israel
4. Notice how cards layer on top of each other

---

## 🎮 Interactive Features

### 🔊 Audio Playback
- Click the speaker icon next to Hebrew text
- Hear authentic Hebrew pronunciation
- Works on all devices with audio capability

### 🗺️ Visual Maps
- Each card shows an SVG map of the region
- Highlighted in the region's unique color
- Jerusalem has a special pulsing marker

### 📖 Content Sections
Each card contains:
- **Region Name**: English and Hebrew
- **Description**: Geographic and cultural overview
- **Biblical Significance**: Scripture context
- **Archaeological Sites**: Historical discoveries
- **Tamil Translation**: Regional name in Tamil

## 🎨 Understanding the Colors

### 🟢 Emerald/Green = Galilee & Golan
Northern region with lush landscapes and the Sea of Galilee

### 🟡 Amber/Orange = Judea & Samaria
Biblical heartland with ancient cities

### ⭐ Yellow/Gold = Jerusalem
The eternal capital - special golden theme

### 🔵 Blue/Cyan = Coastal Plain
Mediterranean coastline with modern cities

### 🟠 Orange/Red = Negev Desert
Southern wilderness and prophetic significance

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Smooth Scrolling
- Scroll slowly to appreciate the stacking effect
- Each card stays "sticky" at the top as you scroll
- New cards slide up and push previous cards back

### Desktop vs Mobile
- **Desktop**: Content and visuals side-by-side
- **Mobile**: Stacked vertically for easy reading

### Switch Anytime
- Toggle back to Interactive Cards View whenever you want
- Both views show the same rich content
- Choose based on your preference

## ⚙️ Troubleshooting

### Cards Not Stacking Properly?
- Ensure you clicked "Scrolling Stack View" button
- Try refreshing the page
- Check browser compatibility (modern browsers work best)

### Audio Not Playing?
- Check device volume
- Ensure browser has audio permissions
- Try clicking the speaker icon again

### Layout Issues on Mobile?
- Rotate device for better view
- Zoom in/out for comfortable reading
- Switch to Interactive Cards View if needed
